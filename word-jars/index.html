About the game

Six glass jars on wooden shelves, each holding a few word tags. Every word belongs to a hidden group: planets, fruit, big cats, tools and many more. Move the words until each group fills a jar of its own, and the jar seals with a cloth lid and its category's name.

  • 100 levelsFrom gentle first puzzles to boards with tricky words that seem to fit two groups. Every level is checked by a solver.
  • A daily puzzleA new board every day, one try, a stopwatch and a streak to keep. Share your result without spoiling the answer.
  • Hints and undoUndo any move, restart a level, and one free hint per try that names a group still to find.
  • Plays offlineNo account and no sign-in. Your progress stays on your phone.

How to play

  1. 1
    Tap a jar to lift its top word.
  2. 2
    Drop it on a word from the same group, or in an empty jar.
    • VENUS
    • MARS
    • SATURN
    • MERCURY
  3. 3
    Fill a jar with one group to seal it.
    • Planets

Four wrong drops and the level starts over, so look before you drop. Stuck? Watch an optional short video for an extra hint, or to take back your fourth mistake once per try.
