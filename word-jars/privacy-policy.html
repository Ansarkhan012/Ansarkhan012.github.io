1. Who we are

Word Jars: Word Sort Puzzle (“Word Jars”, “the app”) is developed and published by Ansar Games (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information is processed when you use the app and what choices you have. For the data processed by Google AdMob, Google acts as an independent controller; see the linked Google policies.

2. Data stored on your device

Word Jars does not have accounts and does not require you to sign in. The following data is saved locally on your device using Android's app storage and is not sent to us:

  • Levels: the highest level you unlocked, your best stars and fewest moves per level, the board of a level in progress, and whether this try at a level has used its free hint.
  • Daily puzzle: the board of a daily in progress and its time, and for each finished daily whether you solved it, your moves, time, stars and the colored squares used for sharing; your current and best streak.
  • Settings: sound and haptics, and whether you have seen How to Play.

This data stays on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include it in your device backup. We do not operate our own servers and do not receive this data.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Word Jars is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob (including Google's User Messaging Platform for consent):

  • Occasional full-screen ads after you solve a level and move on: never in the first five levels, at most once every three solved levels, and with a minimum time between them. There are no ads on the game board and none in the daily puzzle.
  • Optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch: an extra hint once your free hint is used, or taking back your fourth mistake once per try instead of starting the level over. You only get the reward if you watch the video.

4. Sharing your daily result

When you tap Share after a daily puzzle, the app opens your phone's share sheet with a short text: the puzzle number, colored squares, your moves, time and streak, and a link to the app on Google Play. It contains no words or category names. Where it goes is your choice (for example a messaging app); that app's own privacy policy then applies. We do not receive what you share.

5. Device permissions

Word Jars only uses the permissions needed to show ads, all added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK: internet and network state, the advertising ID and Android's Privacy Sandbox ad APIs, and the background-work permissions the SDK uses (wake lock, foreground service). Haptic feedback can be turned off in Settings. The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

6. Why we process data (legal bases)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, we rely on:

  • Consent — for personalized advertising and related storage/access of information on your device, collected through Google's consent message.
  • Legitimate interests — to provide a free, ad-supported game, measure ads, limit how often they show and prevent fraud.

7. Your choices and opt-outs

  • Ad personalization: on Android, open Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) to delete or reset your advertising ID. If you delete it, apps can no longer use it for personalized ads. You can also manage ad personalization at My Ad Center.
  • Consent choices (EEA/UK/Switzerland): open the app's Settings and tap Privacy options to review or change your ad consent at any time. This button appears whenever Google's consent rules apply to you.
  • Rewarded videos: always optional — simply don't tap the video buttons.
  • Stop all collection: uninstalling the app stops all further data collection by the app.

8. Data deletion

  • Data on your device: clear the app's storage or uninstall the app to delete your progress, results and settings immediately.
  • Data held by Google AdMob: we do not hold advertising data ourselves. Delete or reset your advertising ID as described above, and see Google's Privacy Policy for your options with Google. For any question, email ansarkhanaptech1234@gmail.com with the subject “Word Jars data deletion request” and we will help within 30 days.

EEA, UK and Switzerland: you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to processing (including processing based on legitimate interests), to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time (in the app under Settings → Privacy options) without affecting earlier processing. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

California and other U.S. states: you have the right to know what personal information is collected and how it is used and disclosed, to request deletion and correction, and to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not sell personal information for money. Using AdMob for personalized ads may count as “sharing” under California law; you can opt out by deleting your advertising ID or limiting ad personalization as described in section 7. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.
