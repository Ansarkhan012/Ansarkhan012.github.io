About the game

Pocket Playroom is a little room full of quick games, with Tenny, a bouncy purple robot buddy, cheering you on. Sort colours, hop between planets, flip cards or race through a quiz. Every game takes a minute to learn and is easy to pick up for a short break.

3 daily challengesThree new games to beat every day, with double coins and a streak to keep alive.
Weekly LeagueEarn points from every game and race nine computer players. Top 3 move up, from Bronze to Champion.
2 PlayersPut the phone on the table and play face to face with a friend. No internet needed.
Plays offlineNo account, no sign-in. Your progress stays on your phone.

Ten games in one

Color Sort PuzzlePour the balls until every tube holds one colour.
Word Ladder PuzzleChange one letter at a time to reach the goal word.
Light Path PuzzleTurn the pieces until every tile lights up.
Sum Grid PuzzleMake every row and column add up.
Tap Stack ArcadeTap at the right moment and build the tallest tower.
Orbit Hop ArcadeJump from planet to planet across space.
Block Fit ArcadeFit the shapes and clear full lines.
Lane Dash ArcadeSwitch lanes, dodge the blocks and grab coins.
Memory Flip BrainFind every matching pair before the moves run out.
Quick Quiz Brain364 questions about the world, science and more.

Earn stars, coins and toys for Tenny's shelf. Stuck? Use a hint or an undo, or watch an optional short video for a second chance.
