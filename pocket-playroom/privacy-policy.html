1. Who we are

Pocket Playroom: Mini Games (“Pocket Playroom”, “the app”) is developed and published by Ansar Games (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information is processed when you use the app and what choices you have. For the data processed by Google AdMob, Google acts as an independent controller or as our processor, depending on the processing; see the linked Google policies.

2. Data stored on your device

Pocket Playroom does not have accounts and does not require you to sign in. The following data is saved locally on your device using Android's app storage and is not sent to us:

  • Progress: coins, stars, levels and best scores for each game, daily challenges and streaks, quests, achievements and toys, Weekly League points and tier, and the avatars and room themes you unlocked.
  • Settings: sound, music, vibration, colour-blind symbols, daily reminders and the reminder time.
  • Play statistics: simple counts such as sessions, games played and finished, and how many ads were shown, together with a random install number created on first launch. These statistics contain no name or device ID and are never sent anywhere unless you choose Settings → Share play stats yourself, which opens your phone's share sheet so you decide where they go.

This data stays on your device until you reset it in Settings, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. We do not operate our own servers and do not receive this data.

3. Data collected by Google AdMob

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), including Google's User Messaging Platform for consent. It collects information automatically when ads are requested and shown:

DataPurpose
Advertising ID and app set IDShowing and measuring ads, frequency capping, fraud prevention and, with consent where required, personalized ads
IP address (used to estimate approximate location)Ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention
Device and app information (model, OS version, language, screen size, app version)Showing ads that fit your device, measurement and diagnostics
Ad interactions (ads shown, tapped or watched) and consent choicesAd measurement, rewarding you for a watched video, and respecting your consent
Diagnostic information from the ads SDKKeeping ads working and measuring their performance

We do not sell your personal information for money. We do not use the data above to identify you personally. The app does not use Firebase or any other analytics service.

4. How ads appear in the app

  • Optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch, for example double coins, a bigger daily gift, free coins in the Shop or a second chance in a game. You get the reward only if you watch the video.
  • Occasional full-screen ads when you leave a result screen. Never during your first games, never during play or a tutorial, and with a minimum time between them.
  • There are no banner ads.
  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. When required, the app shows Google's consent message before ads are requested and respects the choices you make there. You can change your choices later in Settings → Ad privacy choices.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services · How Google uses advertising data.

5. Weekly League and 2 Players

League rivals are computer players whose scores are generated on your phone. Your points are never sent to us or to other players, and you are not matched with real people. 2 Players mode is played by two people on the same phone; nothing is sent over the internet.

6. Notifications and sharing

  • Daily reminders: if you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders on your phone, for example when new daily challenges are ready. No notification data is sent to us. Turn them off in Settings or in your phone's settings.
  • Sharing: when you tap Share, the app creates a picture or text of your result on your phone and opens Android's share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you pick an app and send it yourself.

7. Device permissions

Pocket Playroom uses internet and network state (for ads), advertising ID (for ads), notifications (for daily reminders, only if you allow them) and vibration (haptic feedback, can be turned off in Settings). The Google Mobile Ads SDK may also declare permissions it needs to work. The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

8. Why we process data (legal bases)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, we rely on:

  • Consent for personalized advertising and related storage of or access to information on your device, collected through Google's consent message.
  • Legitimate interests to provide a free, ad-supported game, to measure ads and to prevent fraud.

9. Your choices and opt-outs

  • Ad personalization: on Android, open Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) to delete or reset your advertising ID. You can also manage ad personalization at My Ad Center.
  • Consent choices (EEA/UK/Switzerland): open the app's Settings and tap Ad privacy choices. This row appears whenever Google's consent rules apply to you.
  • Rewarded videos: always optional. Simply don't tap the video button.
  • Reminders: turn off Daily reminders in the app's Settings.
  • Stop all collection: uninstalling the app stops all further data collection by the app.

10. Data deletion

  • Data on your device: use Settings → Reset progress, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app to delete your progress, settings and play statistics immediately.
  • Data held by Google AdMob: we do not hold this data ourselves. You can delete or reset your advertising ID as described above, and manage your data with Google through your Google Account. For any question, email ansarkhanaptech1234@gmail.com with the subject “Pocket Playroom data deletion request” and we will help within 30 days.

11. Your rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA)

EEA, UK and Switzerland: you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to processing (including processing based on legitimate interests), to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time (in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices) without affecting earlier processing. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

California and other U.S. states: you have the right to know what personal information is collected and how it is used and disclosed, to request deletion and correction, and to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not sell personal information for money. Using AdMob for personalized ads may count as “sharing” under California law; you can opt out by deleting your advertising ID or limiting ad personalization as described in section 9. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

To exercise any right, email ansarkhanaptech1234@gmail.com. We will respond within the time required by law (generally within 30 days, or 45 days under CCPA).

12. Data retention

  • Google AdMob: retained by Google according to Google's Privacy Policy and its data retention practices.

13. International transfers

Google may process data in the United States and other countries. Google uses appropriate safeguards for international transfers, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

14. Security

Data sent by the ads SDK to Google is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). We do not run our own servers or databases. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect information.

15. Children

If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help delete it.
