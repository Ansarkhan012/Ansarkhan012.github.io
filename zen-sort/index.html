About the game

Tap a tube to lift its top ball, then tap another tube to drop it — onto the same color or into an empty tube. Sort every tube into a single color to finish the level. There's no timer and no pressure: just a quiet moment of order.

  • 2,000 levelsEvery level is checked by a solver, so it can always be solved.
  • Gentle difficultyFrom 3 to 12 colors, with a calmer “relax” level every 7th level.
  • Helpful toolsUndo, restart and one extra tube per level.
  • Calm by designSoft pastel colors, smooth animations, light and dark mode.
