1. Who we are

Zen Sort: Ball Sort Puzzle (“Zen Sort”, “the app”) is developed and published by Ansar Games (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information is processed when you use the app and what choices you have. For the data processed by the Google services described below, Google acts as an independent controller or as our processor, depending on the service; see the linked Google policies.

2. Data stored on your device

Zen Sort does not have accounts and does not require you to sign in. The following data is saved locally on your device using Android's app storage and is not sent to us:

  • Game progress and statistics (current and highest level, levels solved, moves, undos, streaks) and your current unfinished game.
  • Settings (sound, music, vibration, light/dark theme) and whether you purchased Remove Ads.

This data stays on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. We do not operate our own servers and do not receive this data.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of Zen Sort shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the game screen, occasional full-screen ads between levels, and optional rewarded ads that you can choose to watch (for example to get an extra tube, more undos or to skip a level).

  • Advertising ID. AdMob uses your device's Android advertising ID, together with device information and your IP address, to serve and measure ads. The app may also use Android's Privacy Sandbox advertising APIs (Topics and Attribution) where available.
  • Personalized and non-personalized ads. Depending on your choices and location, ads may be personalized (based on your interests) or non-personalized (based on context such as the app and general location). Non-personalized ads still use data for frequency capping, aggregated reporting and to prevent fraud.
  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. When required, the app shows Google's consent message (User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested, and respects the choices you make there. You can change your choices later in the app under Settings → Privacy options. Where applicable, a message for U.S. state privacy laws may also be shown.
  • Remove Ads. If you buy Remove Ads, banner and full-screen ads are no longer shown, and features that otherwise ask you to watch a rewarded ad (extra tube, more undos, skip level) become free.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services · How Google uses advertising data · AdMob and your privacy.

5. Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config

We use Google Firebase to understand how the game is used and to keep it stable. Analytics data is reported in aggregate and is not linked to your name or contact details. Crash reports help us fix problems. Remote Config lets us adjust settings such as ad frequency remotely. Learn more: Privacy and Security in Firebase · Google Analytics data safeguards.

6. In-app purchase (Google Play Billing)

The optional one-time “Remove Ads” purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. Your payment information is handled by Google under its own terms and privacy notice; we never receive your card or bank details. The app stores locally whether Remove Ads is active and can restore it through Google Play. Learn more: Google Payments Privacy Notice.

7. Device permissions

Zen Sort only uses permissions needed for internet access, ads, analytics and in-app purchases (for example: internet and network state, advertising ID, Play Install Referrer and Google Play Billing). The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

8. Why we process data (legal bases)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, we rely on:

  • Consent — for personalized advertising and related storage/access of information on your device, collected through Google's consent message.
  • Legitimate interests — to provide a free, ad-supported game, measure ads, prevent fraud, understand how the game is used (analytics), and keep the app working (crash reporting, remote configuration).
  • Performance of a contract — to provide and restore the Remove Ads purchase.

9. Your choices and opt-outs

  • Ad personalization: on Android, open Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) to delete or reset your advertising ID. If you delete it, apps can no longer use it for personalized ads. You can also manage ad personalization at My Ad Center.
  • Consent choices (EEA/UK/Switzerland): open the app's Settings and tap Privacy options to review or change your ad consent at any time. This button appears whenever Google's consent rules apply to you.
  • Remove ads: buy Remove Ads in the app's settings to stop banner and full-screen ads.
  • Stop all collection: uninstalling the app stops all further data collection by the app.

10. Data deletion

  • Data on your device: clear the app's storage or uninstall the app to delete your progress and settings immediately.
  • Data held by Google services: email ansarkhanaptech1234@gmail.com with the subject “Zen Sort data deletion request”. Because the app has no accounts, please include details that help us locate your data (for example your device model and the approximate dates you used the app). We will delete analytics and crash data we can associate with your app installation and confirm within 30 days. You can also delete your advertising ID as described above.

11. Your rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA)

EEA, UK and Switzerland: you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to processing (including processing based on legitimate interests), to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time (in the app under Settings → Privacy options) without affecting earlier processing. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

California and other U.S. states: you have the right to know what personal information is collected and how it is used and disclosed, to request deletion and correction, and to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not sell personal information for money. Using AdMob for personalized ads may count as “sharing” under California law; you can opt out by deleting your advertising ID or limiting ad personalization as described in section 10. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

To exercise any right, email ansarkhanaptech1234@gmail.com. We will respond within the time required by law (generally within 30 days, or 45 days under CCPA).

12. Data retention

  • On-device data: kept until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • Firebase Analytics: event data is retained for up to 14 months, according to our Analytics settings, and then deleted automatically.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports are retained for 90 days.
  • Google AdMob and Google Play: retained by Google according to Google's Privacy Policy and Google's data retention practices.

13. International transfers

Google may process data in the United States and other countries. Google uses appropriate safeguards for international transfers, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

14. Security

Data sent by the app to Google services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). We do not run our own servers or databases, and we limit the data collected to what the services described above need. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect information.

15. Children

Zen Sort is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will delete it.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when the app or the services it uses change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. For significant changes we will provide notice in the app or on this page.
