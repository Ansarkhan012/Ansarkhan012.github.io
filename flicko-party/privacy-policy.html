1. Who we are

Flicko Party: Party Races (“Flicko Party”, “the app”) is developed and published by Ansar Games (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information is processed when you use the app and what choices you have. For data processed by Google AdMob and Google Play, Google acts as an independent controller or as our processor, depending on the processing; see the linked Google policies.

2. Data stored on your device

The following data is saved locally on your device using Android's app storage. It is not sent to us:

  • Progress: coins, unlocked heroes, skins, emotes and trails, chests and their timers, the daily gift calendar, daily tasks, trophies, Trophy Road rewards and league.
  • Your player name, if you type one (used on your screen and in friends' rooms, see section 6).
  • Settings: sound, music, screen shake, vibration, notifications and graphics quality.
  • Purchase receipts: a receipt code (purchase token) from Google Play for each purchase, so an item is delivered only once and can be restored.

This data stays on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. We do not receive it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), including Google's User Messaging Platform for consent. It collects information automatically when ads are requested and shown:

DataPurpose
Advertising ID and app set IDShowing and measuring ads, frequency capping, fraud prevention and, with consent where required, personalized ads
IP address (used to estimate approximate location)Ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention
Device and app information (model, OS version, language, screen size, app version)Showing ads that fit your device, measurement and diagnostics
Ad interactions (ads shown, tapped or watched) and consent choicesAd measurement, rewarding you for a watched video, and respecting your consent
Diagnostic information from the ads SDKKeeping ads working and measuring their performance

We ask Google to show only ads rated suitable for general audiences (rating “G”). We do not sell your personal information for money and do not use the data above to identify you personally.

4. How ads appear in the app

  • Optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch: revive in a race, double coins, open a chest sooner, try a locked hero for one race, or triple a daily gift. You get the reward only if you watch the video to the end.
  • Occasional full-screen ads only when you leave a results screen, at most once every 2 finished matches and at most once every 2 minutes. Never during a race, never during the tutorial, and never in a new player's first matches.
  • There are no banner ads.
  • Remove Ads (an optional purchase) turns off the full-screen ads. Rewarded videos stay available because they are your choice.
  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. When required, the app shows Google's consent message before ads are requested and respects your choices. You can change them later in Settings → Ad privacy choices.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services · How Google uses advertising data.

5. In-app purchases (Google Play)

You can buy optional items: coin packs, a one-time Starter Pack and Remove Ads. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive your card number, bank details or billing address. Google tells the app which item was bought and gives a purchase token; the app keeps that token on your device (section 2). Google's handling of payment data is described in the Google Payments Privacy Notice. Refunds are handled by Google Play.

6. Friends' rooms on your Wi-Fi

When you create or join a friends' room, the phones in that room connect directly to each other over your local Wi-Fi network. They exchange your player name (up to 10 characters), chosen hero, skin, trail and emote, and your position during the race. This data goes only to the phones in the room, is not sent to us or to any server, and is not kept after the race. There is no text or voice chat.

7. Computer players

Party races and quick races are filled with computer players that run on your phone. They have made-up names and flags and are always marked with a small robot icon on result screens. You are not matched with real people in these modes.

8. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders on your phone, for example when a chest has finished unlocking or a new daily gift is ready. They are created on your device; no notification data is sent to us. Turn them off in the app's Settings → Notifications or in your phone's settings.

9. Planned online features (not active yet)

We plan to add the features below in future updates. They are not active in the current version. Before any of them goes live, we will update this policy, the effective date and the Google Play Data safety section.

  • Online account, weekly leaderboard and cloud save (optional). If you choose to sign in, you give an email address so we can send a one-time login code. We would store your email address, player name, trophies and a copy of your game progress on our game server (hosted by a cloud database provider such as Supabase) so you can see your rank and keep your progress on a new phone. Playing without signing in stays possible. You will be able to delete your account and its data from inside the app.
  • Online multiplayer. To race real players over the internet, the app would send your player name, hero and look, and your position during the race to our game server, which forwards it to the other players in the same race. Race data would not be kept after the race, except basic logs for a short time to fix problems and prevent cheating.
  • Analytics and crash reports. We plan to use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand which parts of the game are played (for example tutorial completed, race started or finished, purchases) and to fix crashes. These services collect an app instance ID, device and app information, approximate location from IP address, in-app events and crash logs. They would not include your name or email address.
  • Sharing your result. A share button that creates a picture of your result on your phone and opens Android's share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you choose an app and send it yourself.
  • Events and Season Pass. Limited-time events and a Season Pass track would use the same on-device progress described in section 2.

10. Device permissions

Flicko Party uses internet and network state (for ads, purchases and friends' rooms), Wi-Fi state (to find the room on your local network), advertising ID (for ads), billing (for Google Play purchases), notifications (for reminders, only if you allow them) and vibration (haptic feedback, can be turned off in Settings). The Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google Play Billing library may also declare permissions they need to work. The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

11. Why we process data (legal bases)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, we rely on:

  • Consent for personalized advertising and related storage of or access to information on your device, collected through Google's consent message.
  • Performance of a contract to deliver items you buy.
  • Legitimate interests to provide a free, ad-supported game, to measure ads and to prevent fraud.

12. Your choices and opt-outs

  • Ad personalization: on Android, open Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) to delete or reset your advertising ID. You can also manage ad personalization at My Ad Center.
  • Consent choices (EEA/UK/Switzerland): open the app's Settings and tap Ad privacy choices. This button appears whenever Google's consent rules apply to you.
  • Rewarded videos: always optional. Simply don't tap the video button.
  • Notifications: turn them off in the app's Settings.
  • Friends' rooms: only used when you create or join one.
  • Stop all collection: uninstalling the app stops all further data collection by the app.

13. Data deletion

  • Data on your device: clear the app's storage (Android Settings → Apps → Flicko Party → Storage) or uninstall the app to delete your progress, settings and purchase receipts immediately. Bought items that Google Play lets you restore (Remove Ads, Starter Pack) can be restored with the Restore button in the shop.
  • Data held by Google: we do not hold AdMob or Google Play data ourselves. You can delete or reset your advertising ID as described above and manage your data through your Google Account.
  • For any question, email ansarkhanaptech1234@gmail.com with the subject “Flicko Party data deletion request” and we will help within 30 days.

14. Your rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA)

EEA, UK and Switzerland: you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to processing (including processing based on legitimate interests), to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time (in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices) without affecting earlier processing. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

California and other U.S. states: you have the right to know what personal information is collected and how it is used and disclosed, to request deletion and correction, and to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not sell personal information for money. Using AdMob for personalized ads may count as “sharing” under California law; you can opt out by deleting your advertising ID or limiting ad personalization as described in section 12. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

To exercise any right, email ansarkhanaptech1234@gmail.com. We will respond within the time required by law (generally within 30 days, or 45 days under CCPA).

15. Data retention

  • On-device data: kept until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • Friends' room data: not kept after the race.
  • Google AdMob and Google Play: retained by Google according to Google's Privacy Policy and its retention practices.
  • Planned online features: account and leaderboard data would be kept until you delete your account; crash reports and analytics according to Firebase's retention settings (we will state the period here before launch).

16. International transfers

Google may process data in the United States and other countries. Google uses appropriate safeguards for international transfers, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

17. Security

Data sent by the ads and billing SDKs to Google is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Friends' rooms stay inside your local network. The app currently does not use our own servers or databases. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect information.

18. Children

Flicko Party is a family-friendly game intended for a general audience aged 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help delete it.

19. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when the app or the services it uses change, for example when a planned feature from section 9 is released. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. For significant changes we will also tell you in the app.
