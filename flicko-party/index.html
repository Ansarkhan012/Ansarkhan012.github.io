About the game

Flicko Party is a bright, silly 3D party game. Pick a hero, a samosa, a cup of chai, a cricket ball or even a pressure cooker, and run through kitchens, rooftops, wedding halls, bazaars and cricket grounds full of rolling pins, swinging lamps and crumbling plates. Jump, dodge, use your hero's power and be the last one standing. Between races, flick your heroes at a target with friends on the same phone.

8-player party racesThree rounds on random maps. Only the fastest go through: 8 → 5 → 3 → 1.
Hero powersSteam Boost, Roll Dash, Spin Dodge and Steam Push, each with a fair cooldown.
Flick with friends2 to 4 players take turns on one phone, or play against a bot.
Rewards every dayA 7-day gift calendar, daily tasks, chests and a Trophy Road to climb.

Game modes

8→ 5→ 3→ 1
Party8 racers, 3 rounds, random maps. The top 5 go to round 2, the top 3 race the final. Fall three times and you're out, or watch a short video to revive right where you fell.
Quick racePick a map and a difficulty, Easy, Hard or Insane, and race 4 runners. Grab stars on the way for extra coins.
FlickDrag back, aim and flick your hero at the target. 2 to 4 players pass the phone, or play 1 vs a bot. Three rounds, closest to the middle wins.
Friends roomPhones on the same Wi-Fi join with a short room code and race together. Empty seats are filled with bots.

New players get a short, friendly tutorial first. It can be skipped.

Hero powers

Light, fair powers with a cooldown. Tap the round button above Jump. Bots use them too, and we tested them in hundreds of simulated races so no hero is too strong.

Fair and friendly

  • Bots are always marked. Computer racers have a small robot icon on results, so you always know who is a bot.
  • Videos are your choice. Rewarded videos (revive, double coins, chest skip, hero trial, triple gift) only play when you tap them.
  • No ads while you play. A full-screen ad can appear only when you leave a results screen, at most once every 2 matches and 2 minutes, and never in your first matches. Remove Ads turns them off for good.
  • Optional purchases. Coin packs, a Starter Pack, the monthly Season Pass and Remove Ads. Heroes, skins and coins can all be earned by playing too.
  • No chat, no strangers. There is no text or voice chat. Friends' rooms work over your own Wi-Fi.
