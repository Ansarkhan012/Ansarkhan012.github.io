About the game

Drag glossy blocks from the wooden shelf onto an 8×8 board. Fill a whole row or column and it bursts into flower petals. Plan your space, chain clears into combos and keep the garden blooming.

Easy to learnDrag, drop, clear. No timer and no rush in Classic.
Combos & bonusesClear lines in a row for Combo ×2, ×3… and empty the board for a Perfect Bloom.
Cozy wood & gardenHand-crafted wooden look, glossy blocks, petal effects and a night-garden dark mode.
Plays offlineNo account, no sign-in. Your progress stays on your phone.

Two ways to play

Classic Endless

Score as high as you can and beat your best. Your game is saved after every move, so you can continue any time.

Levels 100 levels

Reach each level's goals within a limited number of moves and earn up to three stars. Every level unlocks the next.

Level goals

Collect flowersFlower blocks bloom away when their row or column is cleared.
Reach a scoreEarn points for every block and much more for lines and combos.
Clear linesClear a number of rows and columns before your moves run out.
Break stonesClear a stone's line to break it. Hard stones in later levels need two clears.

Out of moves or out of room? Watch an optional short video for +5 moves or a second chance, once per try.
